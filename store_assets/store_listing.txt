BoxesBenBoxes

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (Max 80 characters) ===
Classic restaurant placemat dot game! Play family, friends, or 4 smart AI tiers.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (Up to 4000 characters) ===
Remember sitting at a restaurant diner booth with family, waiting for your food, armed with crayons and a kids' menu or paper placemat? 

BoxesBenBoxes (also known around the world as Dots and Boxes, Boxes, Pigs in a Pen, or the Dot Game) brings that pure, nostalgic pen-and-paper experience to your phone and tablet. No crumpled napkins, no running out of ink, and no unevenly drawn dot grids!

🎓 LOVED BY TOP STUDENTS & THINKERS
Loved by students, engineers, and strategists, BoxesBenBoxes is much more than simple entertainment. It encourages spatial observation, pattern recognition, concentration, and multi-move forward planning. Every line you draw either sets a trap or leaves an opening for your opponent!

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• PURE PASS-AND-PLAY FUN: Designed for 2 to 4 players sharing a single device. Look up, laugh, and play with your kids, parents, grandparents, friends, or siblings right across the table.
• 4 SMART COMPUTER PERSONALITIES: Playing solo? Test your wits against 4 distinct AI tiers:
  1. Random Dude — carefree, unpredictable moves for beginners.
  2. Sees Boxes Dude — snaps up any box with 3 sides already drawn.
  3. Somewhat Crafty — avoids giving away the 3rd side of a box whenever a safe line exists.
  4. Minimizer Dude — calculates chain sacrifices to give away the fewest boxes and control the endgame!
• INSTANT GRID PRESETS: Start playing in seconds! Choose from Quick (3×3 dots / 4 boxes, ~2 min), Classic (4×4 dots / 9 boxes, ~5 min), Standard (5×5 dots / 16 boxes), Challenge (6×6 dots / 25 boxes), or build a custom grid size.
• CUSTOM INITIALS & CRAYON COLORS: Personalize each player with their own name, initial stamp, and classic restaurant crayon palette (Crayon Red, Royal Blue, Forest Green, Warm Orange, and Berry Purple).
• SMOOTH & FORGIVING TOUCH: Generous line hitboxes mean no frustrating mis-taps on small mobile screens. Tapping between dots is effortless for both young kids and adults.
• OPTIONAL UNDO: Toggle Undo ON in setup for friendly learning matches, or keep it OFF for authentic "once you make your mark, it stays" rules.
• LOCAL SCOREBOARD & LIVE WORLD MATCH TRACKER: Keeps a clean record of your past matches and syncs completed games to the live global placemat feed at https://jeffmauldin.github.io/BoxesBenBoxes/
• RESPECTFUL, FAMILY-FRIENDLY EXPERIENCE: Kid-safe, G-rated, and never interrupts mid-game. A 4-second post-game celebration pause with a 55-piece confetti shower lets everyone enjoy the victory banner and box tally.

Rediscover the timeless restaurant tradition that entertained generations before smartphones existed. Grab a friend, tap your first line, and see who can claim the most boxes!
