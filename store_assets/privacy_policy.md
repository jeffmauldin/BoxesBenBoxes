# Privacy Policy for Boxes: Classic Dot Game

**Last Updated:** October 2026

**jamsoft68** ("we", "us", or "our") built the **Boxes: Classic Dot Game** app as an ad-supported game. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone deciding to use our app.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**Boxes: Classic Dot Game** does not require any account creation, user registration, email address, or phone number. All match history, player names, and game preferences are stored locally on your device via standard local app storage (`AsyncStorage`) and are never transmitted to our servers.

### 2. Advertising and Third-Party Services
This app utilizes third-party advertising services provided by **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive banner and occasional interstitial advertisements. 

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, such as:
- Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
- Diagnostic and crash performance logs.
- General coarse location derived from IP address (to serve geographically appropriate advertisements).

For more information on how Google processes advertising data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google Uses Information From Apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### 3. Children’s Privacy
Our game is designed to be family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal data has been inadvertently submitted, please contact us.

### 4. Data Security
Because no personal user data or game history is uploaded to external servers operated by us, your personal match records remain entirely on your device and can be cleared at any time directly through the in-app "Reset" feature or by uninstalling the application.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at your developer contact email listed on Google Play Console (`jamsoft68`).
