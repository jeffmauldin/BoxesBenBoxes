BOXESBENBOXES PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

BoxesBenBoxes ("the App") is developed by Jeff Mauldin Software ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection and usage.

1. Information We Collect

BoxesBenBoxes is designed as an offline-first restaurant placemat game. We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as real names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or precise location).

  • Match Outcome Data (World Match Feed): When you finish a match while connected to the internet, the App transmits a compact game summary (grid dimensions, final box layout, player scores, and your self-chosen community handle or random device identifier) to our cloud database to display on the live public match tracker website. If you are offline, matches are not queued or stored.
  • Local Preferences: The App stores your game preferences (grid size, player names, colors, and community handle) locally on your device via standard local storage.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The App displays advertisements using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

  • AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and coarse diagnostic data to serve ads.
  • Child & Family Protection (COPPA): BoxesBenBoxes is designated for all ages and family play. Ads requested by the App are tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA compliant) and restricted to General Audiences (G-rated content) with non-personalized ads.
  • For more details on how Google handles advertising data, review Google's Advertising Policies.

3. Children’s Privacy

BoxesBenBoxes does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All gameplay functions are designed to operate without any personal account registration or submission of personal data.
